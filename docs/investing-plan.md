## Principles agreed
1. **Order of operations:** pay off high-interest debt, then an emergency fund (target 6 months of
   expenses) in liquid MXN instruments, then long-term investing.
2. **Don't trade the news.** September shows why: the "superpeso" consensus lost 6% in a month.
   Nobody times this reliably. Use rules, not forecasts.
3. **Biggest hidden risk is concentration:** job, income, home and savings are all in Mexico/MXN.
   Long-term money should be global and mostly in USD assets, bought gradually (monthly).
4. **Cheap and boring:** broad index ETFs via the SIC, few trades, no high-fee funds.
5. Keep investing separate from betting. Investing is not the place for "action".

## Avoid
- Individual stock picking, crypto, leverage, options, theme ETFs chasing headlines (AI, oil at $100).
- GBM funds with 1–2.75% annual fees, and Smart Cash Dólares (1.5% + IVA a year).
- Frequent trading: 0.25% + IVA per trade adds up.
- PPR for now: tax-deductible, but locked until 65. Revisit once the job situation is clear.

## Tax notes (Mexico residency)
- SIC/BMV stock gains: 10% ISR on net gains; GBM issues the annual tax statement (constancia).
- Foreign dividends are withheld at source; declare them in the annual return.
